# FlickWaySurfer privacy policy

_Last updated: 19 October 2026_

FlickWaySurfer does not collect, sell, share or send any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no servers.

## What it stores

The floating window's size, position, whether it is shown, the Fill/Fit choice, and the YouTube video you chose in its settings (if you chose one). These are kept in Chrome's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your own computer, never leave your browser, and are deleted when you remove the extension.

## What it reads

The extension runs only on `flick.docchula.com`. There it checks whether a lecture video player is on screen, and where the lecture's picture is, so it can show the window and place it. It does not read lecture content, your Flick account or anything else on the page.

## Third parties

The video, Subway Surfers gameplay or the one you chose, plays in YouTube's embedded player, loaded from `youtube-nocookie.com`. That player is YouTube's, and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) covers it. The extension itself makes no network requests.
