# FlickWaySurfer privacy policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

FlickWaySurfer does not collect, sell, share or send any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no servers.

## What it stores

The floating window's size, position, whether it is shown, the Fill/Fit choice, the channel you're on, the YouTube video you pasted in its settings (if you pasted one), and whether it matches the lecture's speed. These are kept in Chrome's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your own computer, never leave your browser, and are deleted when you remove the extension.

## What it reads

The extension runs only on `flick.docchula.com`. There it checks whether a lecture video player is on screen, where the lecture's picture is, and (if you switch speed matching on) how fast the lecture is playing, so it can show the window, place it and match its speed. It does not read lecture content, your Flick account or anything else on the page.

## Third parties

Every channel's videos, and any video you paste, play in YouTube's embedded player, loaded from `youtube-nocookie.com`. That player is YouTube's, and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) covers it. The extension itself makes no network requests.
